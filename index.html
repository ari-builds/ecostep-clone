Why the name

Eco is not a sticker on the box. It is how we build.

Most flooring gets sold on looks alone. We sell it on three other things too: what you breathe in your own home, what ends up in a landfill, and how many decades it lasts.

  • FSC-certified hardwood only. Every plank traces back to a forest that gets replanted, and we can show you the chain of custody paperwork.
  • Low-VOC, water-based finishes. No solvent smell for a week. You sleep at home the same night we finish.
  • 90% of torn-out flooring gets reclaimed or recycled. Old oak becomes new stair treads. Vinyl goes to a recycler in Charlotte, not the county dump.
  • Works in the house you have. Radiant heat, concrete slabs, 1920s subfloors. We match the system to your home instead of forcing one product on everyone.

A single room is usually one day. A whole main floor is two to four days. Refinishing takes three days plus one night before the furniture goes back. We give you the exact schedule with the quote and we call the evening before if anything shifts.

Honest answer: vinyl plank for kitchens, mudrooms and basements, and hardwood for the rooms you want to still love in thirty years. Hardwood will scratch, but a refinish erases every mark. Our sanders are hooked to a vacuum system that captures over 95% of dust at the drum. We seal doorways and vents on top of that. You will wipe a light film off one or two surfaces, not shovel it out of the cupboards.

About 90% of what we tear out gets a second life. Sound hardwood is denailed and reused for stair treads and repairs. Vinyl and laminate go to a recycler in Charlotte. Carpet pad is recycled locally. The rest goes to the landfill, and we tell you exactly what that was.
